## 5. Payment infrastructure reality (CRITICAL)

### PayPal supported currencies — verified

PayPal supports **25 currencies**. From the [PayPal Developer Currency Codes page](https://developer.paypal.com/api/rest/reference/currency-codes/):

> AUD, BRL, CAD, CNY, CZK, DKK, EUR, HKD, HUF, ILS, JPY, MYR, MXN, TWD, NZD, NOK, PHP, PLN, GBP, RUB, SGD, SEK, CHF, **THB**, USD

- **THB**: ✅ supported
- **IDR**: ❌ **not supported**
- **VND**: ❌ **not supported**

Cross-check: [PayPal Supported Currencies docs](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/reports/reference/paypal-supported-currencies/). Indonesia/Vietnam buyers can in some cases pay PayPal in USD if they have a USD-funded card, but **IDR/VND are not settlement currencies and cannot be displayed as price** through PayPal — confirmed via [Quora answer citing PayPal IDR limitations](https://www.quora.com/Can-PayPal-accept-payment-of-rupiah-currency).

This is the single hardest constraint of the project. PayPal-only checkout effectively prices ID/VN out unless users have international cards.

## 7. Cultural fit for Korean saju concept

### Thailand 🇹🇭

- ดูดวง is a **daily ritual**, not occasional — Sanook has built a top-10 national portal partly on it; horoscope.sanook is 99.2% mobile, indicating habituation, not novelty — [Sanook Horoscope mobile share](https://www.similarweb.com/website/horoscope.sanook.com/).
- The dominant Thai system is **ราศี (rāsī, Vedic-derived 12 zodiac signs)** plus Buddhist/animist date practice. Not Chinese Four Pillars natively.
- Korean saju (사주, Four Pillars / 사주팔자) translates **partially**: K-content soft-power means Thais are receptive to Korean-branded products, but the calculation system (Heavenly Stems / Earthly Branches) is unfamiliar to most users vs. ราศี — [Bestofkorea saju guide explaining the unfamiliar system](https://bestofkorea.com/saju-reading-guide-to-korean-fortune-telling/), [k-saju.co.kr saju vs Western](https://k-saju.co.kr/saju-vs-western-astrology/).
- **Translation strategy**: position as "Korean K-celebrity-style ดูดวง" rather than direct saju-jargon import. K-pop fan adjacency is a real wedge — [Creatrip celebrity Saju compatibility](https://creatrip.com/en/spot/13794).

### Indonesia 🇮🇩

- Religious context is the dominant variable. **MUI Fatwa No. 2 (2005)** explicitly declares ramalan ('iraafah) and perdukunan haram — [HalalMUI](https://halalmui.org/bolehkah-mempercayai-ramalan-dan-perdukunan/), [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/gaya-hidup/20220513102752-284-796303/percaya-ramalan-dan-dukun-hukumnya-dosa-dan-haram-dalam-islam), [Detik Hikmah](https://www.detik.com/hikmah/khazanah/d-8099372/hukum-percaya-ramalan-dalam-islam-dan-bahayanya), [muslim.or.id zodiak ruling](https://muslim.or.id/7970-hukum-membaca-ramalan-bintang-zodiak-dan-shio.html), [Tempo](https://www.tempo.co/ramadhan/larangan-mempercayai-ramalan-menurut-islam-497717).
- Indonesia is **~87% Muslim** — [Indonesia religion data via Statista/Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Indonesia), [Pew Research 2024 Indonesia facts](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2024/03/28/5-facts-about-muslims-and-christians-in-indonesia/).
- Addressable audience for Korean saju concept is therefore the **non-Muslim ~13%**: ~10.45% Christian + ~1.7% Hindu (Bali) + ~0.7% Buddhist + Chinese-Indonesian (~3.28M Tionghoa estimated 2020) — already culturally aligned with Chinese fortune-telling — [Wikipedia Chinese Indonesians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesians), [Britannica Indonesia ethnic](https://www.britannica.com/place/Indonesia/Chinese-and-other-Indonesian-peoples).
- Practically: a national-scale Indonesian saju play has **~30M-40M addressable users** (Christian + Tionghoa + secular Muslim curiosity-tier), which is still huge — but requires careful positioning ("ramalan budaya Korea / K-zodiak") to avoid haram framing. Local primbon apps coexist with Islamic majority because they're framed as Javanese cultural heritage rather than divination.

### Vietnam 🇻🇳

- Vietnam adopted **Four Pillars of Destiny** from China during Tang-Song; same root system as Korean saju — [Hanoi Local Tour zodiac elements](https://www.hanoilocaltour.com/vietnamese-zodiac-elements/), [Wikipedia Vietnamese zodiac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_zodiac), [Wikipedia Chinese zodiac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_zodiac).
- Tử vi is **decision-grade**: Vietnamese consult astrologers before marriage, building, opening a business — [Facts and Details Vietnam superstitions](https://factsanddetails.com/southeast-asia/Vietnam/sub5_9d/entry-3381.html).
- Confucian/folk-Buddhist substrate is closest of the three to Korean's — [k-saju.co.kr on Confucian integration](https://k-saju.co.kr/), [Qiora BaZi → K-Saju](https://qiora.app/blog/k-saju-what-korea-did-with-chinas-four-pillars).
- **Strongest cultural product-fit of the three.** Korean saju ports almost 1:1; main work is glossary translation (천간/지지 → can/chi).
