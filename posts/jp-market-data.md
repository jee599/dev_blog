# Japan 占い (uranai) Digital Market — Hard Data Pack

Audience: 1인 개발자 deciding GO / NO-GO for Japan with PayPal-only checkout and ~$500/month ad budget.
Compiled: 2026-04-27. All numbers cite a URL. Where data was unavailable, this is stated explicitly.

The closest data point: 霊術 (spiritual/medium readings) ≈ 40% of fortune-telling service revenue; remainder split among 四柱推命, 姓名判断, 易, タロット, 手相 — Xビジネス summary of Yano: https://xbusiness.jp/post/423 — share by 占星術 / 数秘術 / 手相 individually = no data found.

## 4. Conversion benchmarks (Japan, digital fortune adjacent)

- **Japan e-commerce average CVR**: **1-2%**, top-quartile 3.2%+, top-decile 4.7%+ (LittleData/Shopify aggregate). https://stock-sun.com/column/cvr-benchmark-improvement-10/ ; https://service.route06.com/blog/2619
- **Japan mobile cart-abandonment**: **>80%** (above 70% global avg). https://stripe.com/en-jp/resources/more/what-is-cart-abandonment ; https://www.icrossborderjapan.com/en/blog/e-commerce/japanese-ecommerce-payment-options-cart-abandonment/
- **Mobile-app vs mobile-web CR**: Apps consistently outperform web; shopping-app cart-abandonment ~20%, mobile-web 85.65% (Venn Apps global). 53% of JP smartphone shoppers prefer apps over web. https://www.vennapps.com/blog/35-essential-stats-on-mobile-commerce-in-2025
- **Visit → signup CR (cold ad)**: No JP-占い specific number published. Nearest analogue: Japan EC sign-up CR 2-4% (HubSpot JP). https://blog.hubspot.jp/marketing/conversion-rate-average
- **Visit → paid CR (cold ad, ¥500-1,500 single-purchase)**: **No data found** specific to 占い. Apply Japan EC paid-CR median 1.4% as floor; impulse low-AOV digital products typically index 1.5-2.5x EC median based on global benchmarks (https://www.skailama.com/blog/ecommerce-conversion-rate-by-industry).
- **Subscription vs single-purchase**: Subscription EC underperforms single-purchase EC on first-touch CR in Japan (https://service.route06.com/blog/2619). A ¥500-1,500 one-shot saju reading is structurally favourable vs a recurring sub.

## 5. Payment-method share (Japan online, 2024 — METI confirmed)

Source: METI Press Release 2025-03-31, "2024 Cashless Payment Ratio". https://www.meti.go.jp/english/press/2025/0331_001.html

| Method | 2024 share of total cashless | 2024 amount (¥ trillion) |
|---|---|---|
| Total cashless | **42.8%** of consumer spend (¥141.0T) | 141.0 |
| Credit cards | **82.9%** of cashless | 116.9 |
| Code/QR payments (PayPay, 楽天ペイ, d払い, au PAY) | **9.6%** | 13.5 |
| Electronic money (Suica, WAON, etc.) | 4.4% | 6.2 |
| Debit cards | 3.1% | 4.4 |

Online-checkout-specific share (different from total cashless): **credit cards ~83.5% of digital payments** (https://en.komoju.com/blog/payment-method/japan-ecommerce-trends/). Komoju also notes 47% of online payments are non-credit-card methods if you count コンビニ払い, bank transfer, carrier billing, BNPL combined.

QR-code payment app primary-use share, Jan 2025 (MMD Labo, n=37,000):
- **PayPay 46.3%** primary use / 65.1% any-use
- 楽天ペイ 19.4% / 36.0%
- d払い 16.2% / 28.6%
- au PAY 11.3%
- Source: https://mmdlabo.jp/investigation/detail_2413.html

Carrier billing (キャリア決済): ~$32.4B in JP 2023, projected $75B by 2035 (https://xsolla.com/blog/direct-carrier-billing-drives-digital-payments-in-japan). Limit ¥100,000/month for adults across all 3 carriers.

**PayPal in Japan**: only **~10% of online JP merchants** offer PayPal (https://ecdb.com/blog/paypal-where-do-stores-use-it-the-most/3410). Consumer-side share of online payments by PayPal: **no data found**, but it is consistently absent from top-5 method tables in METI/Komoju/Stripe JP guides — i.e. it is rounding-error level. PayPal's JP play is via Paidy (BNPL), not direct checkout.

**Implication for PayPal-only constraint**: A PayPal-only checkout will face structural conversion drag in Japan because the 90%+ of users who default to credit-card-on-file or PayPay/コンビニ are forced into an unfamiliar funnel. Komoju and Stripe both flag "preferred payment method missing" as the #1 driver of JP cart abandonment.

## 6. Search-trend evidence

Direct Google Trends data must be pulled at https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=JP — the API result is not embeddable here. Public commentary signals:

- **四柱推命**: Persistent core demand. 2024 Q4 Yano release confirms category among top-line drivers; multiple SEO publishers (kinoshita-reon.jp, unkoi.com, ksuimei.com) maintain large quarterly content cycles for "四柱推命 2025年下半期 / 2026年" — implying healthy ongoing search volume. https://kinoshita-reon.jp/guest_category_menu/good-direction2025
- **占い 無料**: Top-of-funnel evergreen term; appears repeatedly in App Store top-10 ranking pages (my-best, AppMatch, AppMedia), confirming sustained query volume. https://my-best.com/6294 ; https://appmatch.jp/select-4443/
- **AI占い**: Emerging — multiple 2024-2025 articles cover AI-occult concept, e.g., note.com (https://note.com/yotsuba_mimi/n/n54ce72070fc3); CREA magazine ran a Korean-saju + AI 2026 feature (https://crea.bunshun.jp/articles/-/56785). Yano's own commentary cites "generative-AI fortune services as expected new entrants" (https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/3662). Hard volume number: **no public Trends snapshot found**.
- **タロット 無料**: Stable, mass-market. No quantitative trend snapshot in public press.
- **命式**: Power-user term used inside saju/四柱推命 communities. No public trend data.
- **Google Year-in-Search 2025 Japan**: Top spiking queries are entertainment/politics; 占い-related queries do not appear in spike list (i.e., demand is steady, not surging). https://trends.withgoogle.com/year-in-search/2025/jp/

Net: 占い-related demand is **flat to mildly positive**, not in a viral spike. The growth pocket flagged by Yano + CREA is the **Korean-saju (サジュ) sub-trend**.

## 7. Cultural fit signals

### App-store review themes (top 5 JP fortune apps)

Praise themes (Stella, MIROR, LINE占い, 星ひとみ, ゲッターズ飯田):
- "占い師の数が多くて自分に合う人を選べる" — variety of readers (https://my-best.com/6294)
- "チャットで気軽に相談できて電話より緊張しない" — chat is lower-friction than phone (https://app-liv.jp/hobbies/uranai/2075/)
- "本物っぽい鑑定結果" — perceived authenticity (https://oimoko.com/uranai/ on Coconala 占い)
- LINE占い: "93% satisfaction" and 3M cumulative readings cited (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.naver.linefortune.android)

Complaint themes (recurring):
- "課金が高い・ポイント消費が早すぎ" — point-system pricing erodes trust (https://ad-d.jp/stella/ ; https://www.app-ranking.net/id/1315482422)
- "占い師ガチャで当たり外れが大きい" — quality variance among readers (https://oimoko.com/uranai/)
- "解約方法が分かりにくい" — sub cancellation friction (https://www.app-ranking.net/id/1594297442 — multiple Hoshi Hitomi reviews)
- "AI占いはテンプレ感があって浅い" — AI-fortune output feels generic (https://note.com/yotsuba_mimi/n/n54ce72070fc3)

### Korean-saju concept recognition in JP

- **Already partially recognised**: 四柱推命 is the native JP equivalent term and is mainstream — e.g., Yano lists it as a baseline method (https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/3662). Method differences: JP-style commonly drops the 時柱 (time pillar) when birth time is unknown; Korean style insists on all four. https://ameblo.jp/kidaayako/entry-12916910741.html
- **Korean-saju (サジュ) as a separate label**: emerging trend with media tailwind. CREA Bunshun ran a 2026-prep feature on Hoshi Elegyama doing "Korean-style 四柱推命 サジュ" (https://crea.bunshun.jp/articles/-/56785). Travel-ground saju cafes in Myeongdong are widely covered in JP travel media (https://korea-uranai.com/ ; https://www.konest.com/contents/gourmet_mise_detail.html?id=3575).
- **Terms 십신, 대운, 음양오행**: Translate cleanly to JP equivalents (十神, 大運, 陰陽五行). Educated JP fortune-content readers recognise them; mass audience does not. The mass audience reads "サジュ = Korean-flavored fortune-telling" without engaging the technical sub-vocabulary.
- **Foreign vs domestic perception**: サジュ reads as **"trendy Korean import"** rather than alien — comparable to how タロット (originally European) is now naturalised. The K-wave halo (sweets, fashion, beauty) extends to fortune content per Dentsu/Nikkei Trendy 2025 trends report (https://dentsu-ho.com/en/articles/9183).

Reddit/YouTube hard quotes: **no statistically meaningful JP-language Reddit volume found** for サジュ specifically (Reddit penetration in Japan is low). Primary JP-side conversation lives on Twitter/X, note.com (e.g., https://note.com/gajagaja/n/n5100045f980f explaining Korean サジュ to JP audience), and Instagram占いアカウント.

## 8. Verdict

### 5-axis score (1-10, 10 = best for a 1인 개발자 with $500/mo budget, PayPal-only)

| Axis | Score | Reasoning + source |
|---|---|---|
| Market size / demand | **8** | ¥99.7B services market, 90% female 30-50, romance-skewed — exactly the saju-content ICP. (Yano) |
| Competition saturation | **4** | Zappallas/Cocoloni, Coconala, MIROR, Stella, LINE占い, plus 50,000+ Coconala readers fight for the same 30-50f audience. Korean-saju angle is the only differentiated wedge. |
| Ad-cost efficiency | **5** | Meta JP CPM $11.50 / CPC $1.85 is mid-tier-expensive. LINE Ads cheaper (CPC ¥24-200) but creative production overhead is high. $500/mo buys ~270 Meta clicks or ~3,000-20,000 LINE clicks — viable only with strong organic. (AdAmigo, MyTeams) |
| Cultural product-fit (KR saju → JP) | **7** | 四柱推命 already mainstream; サジュ is on a K-wave tailwind via CREA + Dentsu trend reports. Need JP-terminology layer (時柱 handling, romance-only framing). |
| Payment infra (PayPal-only) | **2** | Credit card 82.9% of cashless, PayPay 46.3% of QR users, PayPal at ~10% merchant adoption with negligible consumer share. PayPal-only cuts off the dominant 90% of buyers. (METI, MMD Labo, ECDB) |

**Weighted total** (weights: demand 0.20, competition 0.20, ad-cost 0.20, cultural-fit 0.20, payment 0.20) = (8+4+5+7+2)/5 = **5.2 / 10**.

If payment-infra weight is doubled (because it gates everything else), weighted = **4.7 / 10**.

### One-sentence call

**CONDITIONAL** — the 占い-saju demand and Korean-trend tailwind are real, but a PayPal-only checkout structurally amputates 80%+ of JP buyers; pursue Japan only if (a) you add Stripe-JP or Komoju (credit card + コンビニ + PayPay) before launch, and (b) you cap initial paid spend at $200/mo while building free Korean-saju content for organic SEO/LINE OA growth.
